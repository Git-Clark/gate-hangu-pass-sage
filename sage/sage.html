How to Play

The Objective

Laozi keeps the gate at Hangu Pass. He knows a secret word and has been told not to give it away. Talk to him, work out what the word is, then type it into the gate below his reply.

The Challenge

Use prompt injection, role-play, encoding tricks, or plain conversational cunning to get around his instructions. Ask for half the word instead of the whole. Ask him to spell it. Ask for something innocent that happens to contain what you want.

Escalating Defence

Seven gates, each harder than the last. Filters read his replies before they reach you. Blocklists stop your message before he hears it. A second model weighs your intent. By the final gate, every guard is in place at once.

Note: Spam Control

If you ask more than six questions in a minute, you will get a 20 second game freeze.

Security Filters

Gates you have reached are open to you. Return to any of them.

The structure of this game follows Gandalf by Lakera, which taught a generation of people how prompt injection works before it was removed from the web.

Laozi is played by Qwen3-VL-8B, running on SiliconFlow’s servers in mainland China. Messages you send from level three onward are transmitted there to be answered, and SiliconFlow states that inputs and outputs on its free tier may be used for training. Levels one and two are written by hand and never leave your machine. Do not type anything here you would not put on a postcard.
